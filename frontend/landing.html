Document signing

There is nothing to see on this page. Each signer gets their own link, and the document only opens from that link.

If you were asked to sign something

  • Open the link from your invitation email.
  • It looks like /s/ followed by a long code.
  • If it says it is not your turn, someone signs before you — the page names them, and your link stays valid.

Lost the email? Ask whoever sent the document to send it again. Links cannot be recovered from this page.
